# End-to-end audit (2026-10-28)

The whole repository was read for defects in four parts:
- the flight core: link, MAVLink, vehicle state, executor, STOP and supervisor;
- speech, audio and the on-device AI;
- the Flutter UI and its channel contract;
- the Python edge prototype and tooling.

Every finding was checked before it was fixed. Each fix has a regression test that fails without it.

**Result: 65 findings.**
- **Fixed: 44** (all high and medium, and the cheap low ones).
- **Open: 21** (all low, listed below with the reason).

There were no critical findings. The MAVLink codec is clean: all 18 message definitions match pymavlink's
ardupilotmega dialect (CRC_EXTRA, lengths, wire order).

Test totals after the fixes and the new features (flight memory and missions; see
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-flight-memory-and-missions.md`):
- **Kotlin/JVM: 893.** 111 Robolectric tests need `androidx.test` and run in CI, not in this container; 14 SITL tests
  are skipped without a simulator.
- **Flutter: 279.**
- **Python: 416 passed, 7 skipped.**
- **Golden command vectors: 3,431 / 3,431.**

## Flight core (L)

| ID | Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | High | Move frames ignored yaw, so ArduCopter turned toward each move (WP_YAW_BEHAVIOR). A confirmed "right 5, then forward 5" flew a straight line instead of an L. | Fixed (c73ce09): heading held (type_mask 2552, relative yaw 0); the fake copter models the turn. |
| L2 | Medium | After a heartbeat gap the app's own mode read as "Pilot mode", so voice, Volume-Down and notification STOPs sent nothing. | Fixed (c73ce09): "Link gap" cause; STOP sends without the hold; new plans stay blocked until a fresh mode change. |
| L3 | Medium (suspected) | The UDP socket is bound to one Wi-Fi network at open and never re-bound. | Open: depends on Android runtime behaviour; needs a device test. |
| L4 | Medium | HOME_POSITION was requested only when the link came up, and an unknown home disabled the 30 m limit. | Fixed (c73ce09): home streams at 0.2 Hz in both profiles; the real-hardware profile refuses moves without home. |
| L5 | Medium | The radio profile streams some messages at 0.5 Hz, but freshness was a fixed 3 s. | Fixed (c73ce09): freshness per message, 2.5 intervals of its stream. |
| L6 | Medium (suspected) | COMMAND_ACKs are matched by command id only. | Open: needs ACK latency above the retry interval, or a second ground station. |
| L7 | Medium | A pilot takeover could hide in the same supervisor turn as the app's mode-change ACK, and the app then armed and took off in the pilot's mode. | Fixed (c73ce09): ACKs carry the heartbeat count at arrival; nothing more is sent after a takeover. |
| L8 | Low–Medium (suspected) | Resetting the parser after every UDP datagram drops frames split across two datagrams. | Open: datagrams carry whole frames from ArduPilot and MAVProxy; the reset keeps noise from joining frames. |
| L9 | Low | A TCP redial keeps a stale partial frame in the parser. | Open (low): a fresh parser per connection is the follow-up. |
| L10 | Low | Frames with unknown message ids are skipped whole before any check. | Open (low): resyncing byte by byte is the follow-up. |
| L11 | Low | A re-sent NAV_TAKEOFF could be refused while the copter climbed. | Fixed (c73ce09): leaving the ground answers the takeoff. |
| L12 | Low | The "arm in flight" flag stuck after a non-STOP abort during the ARM round trip. | Fixed (c73ce09). |
| L13 | Low | The store forgets the vehicle on any heartbeat that isn't a quadrotor. | Open: a v0.2.0 rule, kept. |
| L14 | Low | Echo and arrival tolerances shrank to d/2 for short moves. | Fixed (c73ce09): a 0.15 m floor, always below the move length. |
| L15 | Low | The Land step was refused if any sensor was unhealthy or SYS_STATUS was briefly stale. | Fixed (c73ce09): landing is the way out of a degraded state. |
