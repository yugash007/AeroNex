## Defects found and fixed

Each fix had failing evidence first. Logs are in the N6 scratch folder (list at the end).

1. **The AI licence notices were missing** (N2 handoff). llama.cpp (MIT) and the Cactus Needle runtime (Apache-2.0) are linked into the arm64 libraries. llama.cpp also brings nlohmann/json (MIT), which the adapters use. None of them had a notice, so `check_android_package.py --require-ai-notices` could not pass.
   - Red: `test/licenses_test.dart` failed with "Expected: contains 'Apache License', Actual: ''".
   - Fix: added `app/assets/licenses/cactus.txt` (the vendored `NOTICE.md` plus `LICENSE`) and `app/assets/licenses/llama_cpp.txt` (the llama.cpp and nlohmann/json MIT texts from the local llama.cpp tree). Both are registered in `registerLicenses()` and listed in `pubspec.yaml`.
   - Result: the checker passes with `--require-ai-notices`, and every APK carries 6 notice files.
2. **An AI model install never finished while voice was loaded, and Remove would hang.** N2 put the AI models in `filesDir/models`, the directory the Vosk store also uses. A Vosk session holds that directory's read lease for its whole life (`VoskNativeModel`). `VerifiedModelStore.publish` needs the write lock, and `AiModelStore.remove` calls `lock()` without a timeout.
   - What happened on the emulator: connected, with Indian English voice loaded. Import on the Needle row stayed at "Importing… 100%" for over 5 minutes. The complete `model-….part` file was never published.
   - The thread dump showed `aeronex-ai-models` waiting in `VerifiedModelStore.publish` (`WriteLock.tryLock`).
   - From the code (not run before the fix): Remove would have blocked the session-channel worker thread for as long as voice was loaded. STOP and Cancel answer on the main thread, so they were not affected.
   - Red: the new `AiModelsTest.anOpenVoskModelNeverHoldsUpAnAiModelInstallOrRemove` holds a Vosk lease on another thread and failed with `TimeoutException`.
   - Fix: the AI models now have their own store, `filesDir/ai-models` (`AiModels.directory`), with its own lock. `AiModels.adoptLegacy` moves AI files an earlier version left in `filesDir/models` (a 0.2.0 LFM download) into the new store; the store verifies them as usual.
   - `theAiModelsShareTheModelsDirectoryWithoutCollidingWithVosk` asserted the old directory. It is replaced by `theAiModelsHaveTheirOwnDirectoryBesideVosk`.
   - Tests: 3 new or changed in `AiModelsTest` (7/7 pass), and `AiModelSetupTest` 4/4. Emulator re-check: with the fixed debug build and Indian English voice loaded, the 0.2.0 LFM file moved to `files/ai-models` at start and showed Installed. Import on the Needle row went from Not installed to Installed in under 4 s. Remove and a second import also worked.
3. **Flaky JVM test** in the first full run: `ClipBenchTest.pttUpToFinalRunsAtRealTimeThroughTheTailAndTheWholeUtteranceReachesTheEngine` measured 45.5 ms, below the test's lower bound of 50 ms.
   - Cause: `ptt_up` is when the last clip frame was actually delivered, while the 60 ms tail stays on the clip's fixed schedule. A late wake-up of that frame shortens the span, and one Windows timer tick is 15.6 ms.
   - Fix (test only, debug bench): the lower bound is now 40 ms, one frame of slack. The class passed when re-run alone, before and after the change.

### The same answers through the app's real Kotlin path (new)

N2 scored the AI path with a Python stand-in for the rules. N6 replays the recorded model answers through the app's **compiled Kotlin classes**:
- `PlanParser.parseText` (rules first, in the utterance's language);
- `AiRouter.route` (the A1/A2 guards, the `Gloss.english` gloss, the AI thread, the warm-up and the budget);
- `LfmPlanner` (output checks and `commandsMatch`), with each planner step read by `PlanParser` and otherwise by `NeedleToolCaller`;
- `kindOf`, `AiGrounding` (A4) and `FlightPlan`.

Only the two models are replaced, by a fake `AiEngine` that returns the recorded output for the exact input text. The app plans on its own gloss, not the corpus's hand-made one. For the 27 Indic utterances where `Gloss.english` differs from the corpus gloss, the planner and Needle were run again on the real gloss text, so the answers belong to the input the app would actually send.

| Path in the app | Exact | Correct reject | Missed (safe) | False / wrong accepts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rules accept the whole utterance (no AI) | 60 | | | 0 / 0 |
| Rules refuse with a specific reason: final, no AI (A2) | | 29 | **8** | 0 |
| AI path (planner → rules or Needle → grounding) | 11 | 4 | 0 | 0 / 0 |
| **Total (112)** | **71** | **33** | **8** | **0 / 0** |

**Result.** 104/112 exact (92.9%): English 74/82 and Indic 30/30. Every negation, out-of-range, unsafe and missing-number request is refused (33/33).

**Changes from the stand-in (7 utterances).**
- Two now pass:
  - `hi-06`: the real parser reads the whole Hindi plan, so the AI is not needed.
  - `en-plain-12` ("hold your position"): the rules read it.
- Five now miss. The real rules refuse them for a specific reason, and A2 makes that final, so the AI never sees them:
  - "um so like go forward three meters please" and "uh hmm go up two meters okay": `"like"` / `"hmm"` is not understood;
  - "the drone should go down by two meters": `"should"` is not understood;
  - "up you go, two meters": say a distance;
  - "backwards, one meter": say a direction.
- Three misses are shared with N2:
  - "so basically just hover for now": `"basically"` is not understood;
  - "after taking off to six meters move left four meters";
  - "before you land, go back two meters": say one command per step.

**What this means.** The AI fallback helps only with phrasings the rules answer with their *generic* "not understood". Examples on the AI path: "come down two meters", "drift right three meters", "two meters to the right", "left by four meters", and multi-step sentences like "go up two meters then forward four meters then hold". Fillers and unknown words get a word-level hint instead.
- This is the strict reading of spec A2, which allows only the generic rejection, and the safer behaviour. It was kept, not changed.
- If the operator wants fillers to reach the AI, unknown-word rejections would have to be added to the AI-eligible set. That is a spec decision.
- "after taking off to 6 meters move left 4 meters" gets "say a plain number, for example: 2.5 m". The message is misleading: the 0.3.0 `RuleIntentEngine` refuses a clause with two numbers as an "unsupported number form". It is safe, because the clause is refused, but the wording should be fixed later.

## Phone steps for the AI path (operator)

These steps are needed because the AI was verified on the host only. You need a 64-bit ARM phone with at least about 3.3 GiB of RAM: Tier L phones say "Not enough memory on this phone". You also need the simulator running on the PC ([connect instructions](../../README.md#connect-to-the-simulator)).

1. **Install** `aeronex-0.4.0-arm64-v8a-release.apk` (or the fat APK): `adb install -r aeronex-0.4.0-arm64-v8a-release.apk`.
2. **Get the AI models.** Open **Menu → Models → AI command help**, then either:
   - **Download** both, on Wi-Fi: the LFM2.5 planner, 229 MB, and the Cactus Needle tool caller, 35 MB; or
   - **Import** them. Copy the files from this PC (`.worktrees/tooling/models/LFM2.5-VL-450M-Q4_K_M.gguf` and `.worktrees/tooling/cactus/needle3.cact`):
     ```powershell
     adb shell mkdir -p /sdcard/Android/data/com.aeronex.app/files/import/lfm-q4 /sdcard/Android/data/com.aeronex.app/files/import/needle3
     adb push LFM2.5-VL-450M-Q4_K_M.gguf /sdcard/Android/data/com.aeronex.app/files/import/lfm-q4/
     adb push needle3.cact /sdcard/Android/data/com.aeronex.app/files/import/needle3/
     ```
     then tap **Import** on each row.

   **Expected:** both rows say **Installed**, and the section no longer says why the AI cannot help. A 0.2.0 LFM download already on the phone moves over by itself.
3. **Connect** to the simulator (Link setup, UDP, PC address, 14550 / 14551). Turn **Fly the drone** on, or stay in dry run.
4. **Try three natural commands** (type them, or hold Talk and say them in English):

   | Say or type | When | Expected |
   |---|---|---|
   | "lift off to three meters then descend one meter" | on the ground | "Thinking…" with Cancel. Then a plan card marked **AI**: "1. Take off to 3 m, 2. Move down 1 m", with "Understood as: take off to 3 meters, then move down 1 meter". CONFIRM flies it |
   | "drift right three meters" | in the air | AI plan "1. Move right 3 m" |
   | "go up two meters then forward four meters then hold" | in the air | AI plan "1. Move up 2 m, 2. Move forward 4 m, 3. Hold" |

   - The first AI request after start-up or after 2 minutes idle may answer *"The AI was loading. It is ready now: say the command again."* Say it again.
   - Also check that these never reach the AI:
     - "do a flip": the AI answers *"The AI found no command it may send in that…"*;
     - "don't move forward three meters": the rules refuse the negation, with no "Thinking…";
     - "um so like go forward three meters please": the rules answer that "like" is not understood. This is final by spec A2 (see above).
   - Press **STOP** while "Thinking…" is shown: the request is cancelled and no plan appears.
5. **Record and send back:**
   - **Latencies:** the time from Send to the plan card, for the first (cold) request and for the later (warm) ones. The `aiPlan`, `aiTools` and `aiTotal` latency entries in **Menu → Diagnostics** hold the same spans.
   - **Memory:** `adb shell dumpsys meminfo com.aeronex.app` after a few AI requests.
   - **Crashes:** `adb logcat -d | findstr /C:"FATAL" /C:"aeronex"` (Windows).
   - **Phone details:** model, RAM and SoC.

Not measured anywhere yet: cold and warm planner latency on a phone, Needle per-step latency against its 0.5 s budget, STOP → TX while a warm-up or plan runs, speech decode latency during a PTT pre-warm, and AI memory.

## Open caveats

**New in 0.4.0:**
- **AI on phones is unverified.** Not checked on any arm64 device:
  - `NativeAiEngine.load()`, the dlopen of `libaeronex_cactus.so`, and the arm64 llama.cpp build (armv8-a baseline, no dotprod: slower but safe);
  - latency, memory and the 6 s total budget;
  - the trim-memory unload and re-warm.

  The host estimate in the [AI evaluation](2026-10-15-ai-planner-eval.md) is that a phone is 4-8 times slower; a cold first request may exceed the 4.5 s planner budget and answer "The AI was loading…".
- **A2 is strict.** Fillers and unknown words get a final word-level hint, and the AI never sees them (8 of 112 corpus utterances). Widening this needs an operator and spec decision.
- **Misleading "say a plain number" message** for a clause with two numbers ("after taking off to 6 meters move left 4 meters"). The rejection is correct; only the wording is wrong (0.3.0 `RuleIntentEngine`).
- **The LFM download URL is not pinned to a revision** (`…/resolve/main/…`). The SHA-256 check protects integrity, but if upstream replaces the file, Download fails the hash check until the profile is updated. Needle is pinned.
- **The LFM2.5 model licence is not shown.** The GGUF metadata says `lfm1.0` (LFM Open License v1.0). The model is not bundled, and the Licenses screen has no notice for it, because no copy of the licence text was on this PC. Check the terms before distributing.
- **The Needle model mapping (about 35 MB) stays for the process lifetime** (no upstream unload API). The LFM (229 MB mmapped) unloads after 120 s idle and on trim memory.
- **The in-app HTTPS download** of voice packs and AI models was not exercised in this run: import was.
- **Telugu IndicConformer on Tier M/H** (CTC with the Vosk-te stop spotter on Tier H, KWS on Tier M) was checked by JVM tests only. The AVD is Tier L.
- **Models screen wording:**
  - on Tier L, the installed Voice STOP pack row shows a check icon next to "Not for this phone";
  - the screen does not yet say that a Telugu pack finishing mid-session is used from the next voice start.

**Still open from 0.3.0:**
- **No real phone measurements:** reference phones R0-R5 pending. The spec 6.2/6.3 Tier gates and the G1 latency targets stay open.
- **Indic accuracy** was measured only on synthetic eSpeak NG clips (hi and te voice STOP missed on them). The hi/te/ta/kn/ml grammar tables are provisional until a native speaker reviews them.
- **The zh-en KWS licence is unconfirmed.** GigaSpeech is the default.
- **MAVLink signing is not wired.** Links are UNSIGNED.
- **The RADIO-profile freshness window is still 3 s.**
- **The real-hardware profile stays locked** (spec 8.4). This build is for the simulator only.
- **`SessionControllerSitlTest`'s wall bound** depends on simulator speed. It passed this time at 0.675x, with 13.6 ms of margin. Follow-up: scale `WALL_MARGIN_MS` by simulator speed.
- **adb pushes need the folder created first** (`adb shell mkdir -p …/files/import/<id>`).
- **Real speech through the emulator microphone is not testable** (digital zeros). Not verified on a phone: AudioRecord with a real voice, Volume-Up PTT, and the Android 14+ microphone foreground-service rules.
- **`setEndpointerDelays` on the stop recognizer** was not re-checked this round.
- **`check_android_package.py`** requires the Vosk notice and, with `--require-ai-notices`, the two AI notices. It does not yet require the sherpa-onnx, ONNX Runtime and IndicConformer notices, although all of them are in every APK.
- **T12 minor items:** the PttBar release race, the microphone foreground-service fallback, the speech-thread snapshot load, and the stop counter on backgrounding.

**Closed since 0.3.0:**
- in-app pack downloads with Models-screen rows, single-flight pack import with a visible "Installing voice pack" state, and the parser warm-up at load (N5);
- the AI licence notices (fix 1).
